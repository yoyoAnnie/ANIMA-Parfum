De facto

Persona: the sensitive child, now grown — who still flinches at the smell of a steakhouse, and has learned its language.
Profile: Aromatic Wine, Earthy Woody
Top: Bergamot, Rose | Middle: Jasmine, Cedarwood | Base: Indonesian patchouli leaf, Oakwood accord, Vetiver

Back to Rooms, Rain & Smoke

You know how you can love a place for years before you figure out what you actually love about it? As a child, I dreaded the steakhouse. The smell hit me before the door even opened — a thick, fermented cloud of wine, wood, and something sharp, something that made my stomach turn. I thought I hated steak. Only when I grew up and started paying attention did I realize what I was actually sensitive to, and it was never the meat at all. It was the wine, dark and tannic on my tongue, sour and sweet at the same time, and the wood of the barrels, and the cork itself, rough in my fingers and stained red from years of holding the wine in. It is a scent of old money and quiet power, of dark wood and macerated grapes, and for years it made me feel like an intruder in a world I didn't belong to. Now I understand it. Now I can sit in that room and breathe it in — the fermentation, the wood, the cork — and feel not like a child who doesn't fit, but like someone who has learned the language. The label says steakhouse, but the truth is the cellar underneath, and once you name the real thing you can never un-taste it.

十、浸软的葡萄，漂白的软木

小时候跟大人去西餐厅，门一推开，那股味道就压过来。我说不清，只觉得闷，喘不上气。大人说“牛排多香”，我不说话，整顿饭都拿餐巾捂着鼻子。

长大以后才慢慢弄明白，我受不了的不是肉。是酒。是那种开了瓶的、放了一会儿的红酒，酸里带一点铁锈味，还有泡过酒的软木塞，湿的，软的，指甲一掐一个印子。再加上餐厅里那些深色木头家具，被暖气烘着，所有的味道搅在一起，变成一团很重的、化不开的东西。

现在偶尔去那样的地方吃饭，我还是会先闻到那一团东西。但我不躲了。我知道那只是酒、木头和软木，不是什么会把人吞掉的怪物。
