Sunday Pillow

Tabula rasa

Persona: the office siren — the woman who runs on caffeine and competence, the one who lives for the one morning the world leaves her alone.
Profile: Fresh Floral, Soft Powdery
Top: Aldehydes, Bergamot | Middle: Iris, Rose | Base: White musk, Sandalwood

Back to Clean Linens & Skin Scents

I wake at ten, and for one terrible, electric second I think: I'm late. Then the soft, cottony truth settles over me like a second duvet. Sunday. The panic drains out of my shoulders, and I bury my face in the pillow, which smells of rose and clean linen and the warm, sun-kissed ghost of my own skin. The white duvet is a cloud; the light through the curtains is a soft, forgiving gold. I don't have to be on. I don't have to straighten my posture or sharpen my voice or be the woman whose heels make everyone in the office sit up. Nothing has been written on the day yet — no messages, no plans, no noise — and for once that doesn't frighten me at all. The rose scent is powdery and gentle, a lullaby for the senses, and I lie there and let the morning belong entirely to me. This is the luxury no one sees: a pillow that smells of nothing but rest, and a whole day that is still possible.

六、星期日的枕头

十点醒的。睁眼那一秒，心里猛地一沉——迟到了？然后看见窗帘缝里透进来的光，白得发懒，不是工作日早上那种催人的白。星期日。我把脸重新埋进枕头里。

枕套是上周换的，玫瑰味的洗衣液，味道已经不浓了，剩下一点粉粉的尾巴，混着头发上的油气和我自己的体温。被子是白的，皱成一团，我蜷在里头，脚趾碰到床单凉的那一面，缩回来，又伸过去。

不用起来。不用说话。不用把背挺直。我就这么躺着，听楼下有人遛狗，链子哗啦哗啦响，听隔壁厨房在煎东西，油锅滋啦一声。这些声音都跟我没关系。这个上午是我偷来的，谁也别想拿走。
