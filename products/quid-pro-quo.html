Narrative Story

You know the deal you make with summer, even if you never say it out loud: you hand over your plans, your schedule, your shoes, and summer hands you back your senses. The Hawaiian sun bled into the Pacific in riots of orange and violet, and I sat in the sand with a piña colada sweating in my hand, coconut cream thick on my tongue and rum warm underneath it, and the waves keeping their slow, hypnotic time against the shore. The air was thick with crushed pineapple and coconut and salt spray, and the salt was drying on my skin until it stung a little in the best way, and I wasn't sure if the dizziness in my chest was the rum or the sunset or the simple, stupid joy of being alive on a beach with nowhere else to be. Now, whenever I catch that pineapple-and-coconut sweetness on a breeze — on a city street, in a candle, in the foam of a cocktail — I am back on that sand. The scent is sun-baked and salt-stained, a reminder that some moments are so perfect they feel like a dream you get to keep. I close my eyes, and the waves are still there. I can hear them.

四、碎椰，盐渍朗姆

夏威夷那天傍晚，我在沙滩上坐了很久。太阳落下去的速度比想象中慢，海面先是金的，再是橘的，最后变成一种很深的紫，像有人把颜色一层一层往里收。杯子里的冰全化了，朗姆的味道淡得只剩一点尾巴，杯底沉着几片碎椰肉。

海风是咸的，吹在脸上黏黏的，头发贴在脖子上。浪一下一下地打，不凶，像有人在很远的地方翻书。我喝掉最后一口，甜和咸混在一起，椰子味很重，重得有点像防晒霜，但我不讨厌。

后来我回到城里，有一次在便利店闻到菠萝椰子味的润唇膏，站在货架前面愣了几秒。不是想念那片海，是想念那种感觉：坐在那里，什么都不用做，天自己会黑，浪自己会来，我什么都不用赶。
