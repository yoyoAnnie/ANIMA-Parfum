Narrative Story

There is a particular loneliness that only comes after you've gotten everything you said you wanted, and if you've ever felt it, you know it doesn't look like sadness from the outside. It looks like a successful deal, whiskey burning slow on the tongue, a cigar between my fingers, my favorite show playing low in a room that has everything in it, and the quiet thought that I have enough money for me and still keep accumulating, for legacy, for the name after the name. In the quiet, I think of old friends, of beach bonfires, of the raw, unmistakable smell of cold woodsmoke clinging to my favorite heavy jacket like first kisses, charred logs glowing dim orange in the dark, and the cozy, grounding residue of a fire I built from scratch with my own hands. No one understands the loneliness at the top, but I share it with my cigar, and as the smoke fades my weakness fades with it. The embers and ashen cedar are a monument to a fire that once burned for other people. Some nights, I would trade every acre for that jacket.

九、余烬与灰雪松

单子签完，对方走了。我坐回车里，没急着开。西装外套搭在副驾驶，袖口沾了一点灰，是壁炉里的。刚才那间办公室里烧着木柴，火烧得不旺，烟比火多，冷木烟的味道钻进羊毛里，一时半会儿散不掉。

我打开车窗，外面是冬天傍晚的街道，车灯连成一条河。我把领带松了松，从手套箱里摸出一支雪茄，没点，就咬着。烟叶的味道干干的，带一点甜，带一点苦。

我想起以前在海边，一群人围着篝火，火快灭的时候，木头塌下去，火星子往上飘，谁往火里扔了一片湿叶子，烟“轰”地起来，呛得大家一边骂一边笑。那时候我没钱，但好像什么都不缺。现在什么都有了，车里只有我一个人，和一根没点的雪茄。
